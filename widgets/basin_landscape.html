The abstention landscape — two ways to lose the same behavior

A marble on a landscape with two valleys: "I don't know" on the left, confident answer on the right. Temperature shakes it. The two mechanism sliders are the point of this widget: they remove the abstention behavior in structurally different ways, and the difference is invisible if you only watch where the marble ends up.

Mass drain raises the floor of the IDK valley — the state stops being worth occupying at all. Inflow gate leaves the valley exactly as deep, but adds a one-way push in the corridor leading to it: the marble can rest there perfectly well, it just never gets there. Drain ≈ what was measured in Qwen; gate ≈ what was measured in Llama.

What this cartoon is not: a transformer has no energy function, no two-dimensional state, and no marble. "Depth" here stands in for how strongly a region retains generation once it is there (persistence); the one-way corridor stands in for how often generation enters it at all (reachability). Those two are separately measurable on a real model — that is Session 6 — and the whole point of keeping them on separate sliders is that the same extinction on screen can come from either one.
